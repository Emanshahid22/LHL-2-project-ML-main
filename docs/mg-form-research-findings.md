# MG form research findings

Researched field sets for the nine placeholder MG templates, gathered from authoritative sources
and then checked by independent verifiers. This is the input to the "Draft field sets from
authoritative sources" story — it is **not** a practitioner sign-off.

Every field carries a confidence:

- **documented** — a source shows the box exists on the form
- **likely** — strongly implied by documented purpose or a sibling form
- **guess** — reasoned only; treat as a question, not a proposal

**Questions for a practitioner**

- THE HEADLINE ISSUE: MG1 is not in the current MG suite. It is absent from the Home Office list of MG forms 'still in use' (June 2026), absent from the CPS National File Standard (DG6, 2020), and absent from the entire 2011 Prosecution Team Manual of Guidance (zero hits for 'MG1' or 'front sheet' across 131 pages). A practitioner must confirm whether the product should carry MG1 at all, or drop it in favour of DCF case-file metadata.
- No specimen or completion guidance for MG1 was located in any source, official or otherwise. Its box layout, box numbering and mandatory boxes are unknown. Every content field above rests on a single unverified secondary snippet.
- Whether any force or non-police prosecuting agency still uses an MG1-style front sheet locally. A training provider published an 'MG1 (NPA)' variant (non-police agencies) as recently as 2019, but the file is not archived and could not be retrieved — a disclosure officer in a non-police agency (HSE, local authority, DVSA) could say whether such a front sheet is still in circulation.
- When and why MG1 was withdrawn, and what (if anything) formally replaced its file-indexing function — a supervisor's file-build certification now sits on the MG3/MG6 rather than a front sheet.
- Whether the repo's current MG1 fields belong on MG1 at all: 'anticipated plea' and 'trial time estimate' are MG5/DG6 case-management data, 'charges' belong on MG4/MG5, and 'officer in the case' appears across MG5/MG6/MG9. A practitioner should confirm the right home for each before this template is presented to a client as an MG1.

### MG2 — Special Measures Assessment (printed title: Witness Assessment for Special Measures)

Research confidence: **high** · proposed fields: **34** (33 documented, 1 likely, 0 guess) · repo today: 9 fields

Independent verification: **usable-as-draft** · fabrication risk **low** · corroborated 33 field(s) · could not corroborate: Pre-recorded cross-examination (s.28)

> I downloaded bbpolice.uk/uploads/MG2.docx myself and extracted word/document.xml. It is a genuine legacy Word form, not a reconstruction: docProps/app.xml carries <Application>Microsoft Office Word</Application>, Pages=3, and core.xml records lastPrinted 2006-08-04. It contains real Word FORMTEXT/FORMCHECKBOX/FORMDROPDOWN fields. 33 of the 34 claimed fields match printed boxes exactly, in the claimed sections: title 'WITNESS ASSESSMENT FOR SPECIAL MEASURES' + '(Anticipated not guilty plea only)'; header 'RESTRICTED (when complete)'/'Not Disclosable'/'MG2'; footer '2010/11'; 'Name of witness:'; URN as exactly four FORMTEXT boxes; separate 'Age:' and 'Date of Birth:'; section 1 with '[*automatically eligible]' and boxes a)-g) verbatim ('a) Youth under 18 *', 'b) Witness with a mental disorder', 'c) Witness with learning disabilities', 'd) Witness with physical disability/ disorder', 'e) Witness in fear/distress about giving evidence', 'f) Complainant in sexual case *', 'g) Weapons offence'); section 2's two boxes including '(seek expert advice if necessary)'; section 3's seven measure checkboxes; section 4's three bullets; section 5 with a Yes/No FORMDROPDOWN and "If 'Yes' please list:"; section 6 Yes/No FORMDROPDOWN; and the six-box Assessing Officer block (Name / Rank / job title & No. / Date / Tel / Mob / E-mail).  The ONE uncorroborated field is real but is NOT on the form, and the research says so itself: 'Pre-recorded cross-examination (s.28)' is marked confidence 'likely' with the evidence line 'Proposed as a gap, not read off a form.' I verified the template has exactly seven measure checkboxes and no s.28 box. That is accurate self-disclosure, not fabrication. I separately confirmed on cps.gov.uk/legal-guidance/special-measures that s.28 pre-trial recorded cross-examination IS in the CPS list of measures, so the 'gap' framing is correct.  The Manual of Guidance mirror (zakon.co.uk) is genuine and supports the guidance-note quoting verbatim, including details a fabricator would not invent: the s.3 heading 'MG2 WITNESS ASSSESSMENT FOR SPECIAL MEASURES' (with the original's typo), 'One form to be used for each vulnerable or intimidated witness.', 'Generally an MG2 need not be completed in an anticipated guilty plea case. If a not guilty plea is entered then an MG2 will need to be completed subsequently.', note 3.1's factor list (matching the helpText for 'Evidence of disorder/impairment' item for item), note 3.2 'It is essential that the witness is asked for their views as any court considering granting a measure must take account of all circumstances of the case and in particular the views of the witness', note 3.3 on section 5 (carer's view, developmental age, grasp of time intervals), plus the specimen annotations 'This is the officer completing the MG2.' and 'Show here the type of witness requiring Special Measures' and 'See Notes 3.1 and 3.2'. The Section 2 NFS table reads 'Where applicable also include MG2** - Special Measures Assessment'. GOV.UK confirms MG02 = 'Special Measures Assessment' and the purpose sentence verbatim. The CPS page confirms both prosecutor-duty quotes, the s.16/s.17(4)/(4A)/(4B) eligibility set, and the record-keeping/inform-the-witness duty.  CITATION DEFECTS (no broken URLs, but two imprecise ones): (1) The nearpod presigned URL is designated 'the primary source for the field set' and credited with box titles read 'directly off the specimen', but it is an image-only scan with no text layer — I fetched all 5.9MB and could extract no text. Interview-record content belongs on MG15. The required-flag proposals are inferences the research correctly flags as needing a practitioner.

**Questions for a practitioner**

- THE REPO'S MG2 IS THE WRONG FORM. The current template calls MG2 'Initial Description of Taped Interview' with tape references and an interview summary. That is MG15 (Interview Record) content. MG2 has never been an interview form; it is the special measures assessment. The whole field set must be replaced, and any existing MG2 drafts will need migrating rather than version-bumping.
- Which boxes a given CPS Area treats as mandatory in practice. CPS guidance only requires prosecutors to 'check it has been fully completed' — it does not define that, and nothing on the form itself is marked mandatory. A disclosure officer or CPS paralegal should settle the required flags proposed above.
- Whether the force's live MG2 is still the 2010/11 version. Both the MoG specimen and the template still in circulation carry 'RESTRICTED (when complete)', a protective marking abolished in the 2014 move to the Government Security Classifications — so a locally re-issued OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE version may exist with different boxes.
- Whether the current form adds boxes the 2010/11 version lacks: s.28 pre-recorded cross-examination; automatic eligibility for modern slavery (s.17(4)), domestic abuse (s.17(4A)) and Schedule 1A violent offences (s.17(4B)); interpreter or language needs; a Registered Intermediary assessment reference; or a Witness Care Unit contact.
- Whether the force records the assessment on an MG2 at all, or inside a special measures screen in its case management system (Athena, Connect, NICHE) that then generates the MG2 — and what the Digital Case File equivalent looks like, given the Home Office confirms DCF will absorb the MG forms.
- Disclosure handling. MG2 is headed 'Not Disclosable' and carries chequered banding, which the Home Office confirms means 'not disclosable to the defence'. A practitioner must confirm that the product excludes MG2 from any defence-facing bundle or export, and whether third-party contact details in section 5 need redacting before the form is served on anyone.
- Whether an MG2 is needed on an anticipated guilty plea. MoG says generally not, but one 'will need to be completed subsequently' if a not-guilty plea is entered — how a given CPS Area chases that in practice, and whether the product should prompt for it on plea change.
- How the MG2 relates to the special-measures boxes on the rear of the MG11 and to the vulnerable/intimidated flags on the MG9 witness list: the same information is captured in three places, and only a practitioner can say which the CPS actually reads.
- Whether 'Age' should be a stored field or derived from date of birth — the paper form prints both, but a digital form storing both invites them to disagree.

**Questions for a practitioner**

- Whether the force in question still uses an MG3 document at all. - The literal box numbering, box order and box headings of the current MG3. The field list above is reconstructed from DG6 Annex 4 (which is the authoritative content specification) rather than read off the form, so labels are ours, not the form's.
- Which fields are mandatory in practice. DG6 says the information "will be proportionate to the issues in the case and the complexity of the case" (4.18) and that failure to provide required material "may cause the referral to be rejected" (4.16) — only a practitioner or the local CPS Area can say which boxes a referral is actually rejected for.
- The current wording and answer options of the two disclosure questions added to the MG3 in June 2019, and whether they carried across into the CM02 / Digital Case File equivalent.

**Questions for a practitioner**

- Which generation of the MG6 the client's users actually hold. Every specimen obtainable publicly is the Manual of Guidance 2010/11 form, headed 'RESTRICTED (when complete) - Not Disclosable'. 'RESTRICTED' was abolished in April 2014 by the Government Security Classifications Policy, and the Home Office (June 2026) describes current MG forms as marked OFFICIAL / OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE with chequered banding. A disclosure officer or CJU needs to confirm whether the in-use MG6 is a re-marked 2010/11 form or a locally revised one, and whether box numbering is unchanged.
- Whether the 'Rationale for police charging decision' page is still used, and against which paragraphs. The specimen cites 'DPP Guidance on Charging (v4)' paragraphs 4 and 18; the current instrument is the Director's Guidance on Charging, sixth edition (December 2020), which rewrote the police-charging provisions. Only a practitioner can say what the equivalent boxes are now expected to record.
- Which boxes are mandatory in practice. The printed form marks only the page 3 remand/intimidation section as one that MUST be completed; sections 1-9 are questions with no asterisks. Local CJU file-quality checklists, not the form, decide what counts as an acceptable MG6 — and HMICFRS has repeatedly criticised MG6 completion quality.
- Whether MG6 content is still entered on the form at all locally, or typed into the police-CPS digital interface. DG6 Annex 5 expects 'Information — provided digitally or MG3/MG6' and disclosure 'via digital message or, in default, by using forms MG3/MG6', and the Digital Case File is intended to absorb the MG forms into case management systems (Home Office guidance, June 2026, targets full operation across forces).
- Whether the force sends MG6 information pre-charge. National Disclosure Standards 2018 footnote 1 says pre-charge provision of MG6 schedules 'is subject to local arrangements', under review by the National File Standards working group; the Home Office guidance repeats that local CPS arrangements may govern MG6 information for charging decisions.
- How the Yes/No boxes should be captured. The PDF specimen has blank answer spaces and the .docx has no dropdown fields, so whether these are two-option selects, tri-state (Yes/No/Not applicable), or free text is a local convention.
- Whether one MG6 per file (Manual of Guidance Note 1) still holds in the client's workflow with multiple defendants, given that page 3 must be duplicated per remanded defendant — i.e. whether the product should model one MG6 with N remand pages, or N forms.
- Whether the MG6 remains genuinely non-disclosable in the client's use. **Questions for a practitioner**

- WHICH VERSION IS IN USE. The June 2026 MG14 template drops the 2013 'review date' finalisation block and the 'Rank & No. / Job title' box. This decides whether the last three fields belong on this template at all.
- WHETHER MG14 BELONGS IN THIS PRODUCT. MG14 is a police out-of-court-disposal record signed in a police station, not a prosecution case-file form a defence solicitor or prosecutor completes, and most forces now generate it from NICHE/custody systems rather than a Word template. Confirm the client's actual user for this form before building it.
- REGIME RISK. Conditional cautions (ss.22–27 CJA 2003) are due to be replaced by diversionary and community cautions under PCSC Act 2022 Part 6, which would repeal the conditional caution provisions. Only s.116 (code of practice power) appears commenced as at August 2026, but commencement must be re-checked at release; the Criminal Justice Hub already describes MG14 as covering 'a conditional caution or equivalent out-of-court disposal record'.
- MANDATORY IN PRACTICE. Which boxes a force will reject the form for leaving blank. Only the s.23(4) CJA 2003 elements (offence details, admission, consent, conditions, signature) are legally load-bearing; Gender, Ethnicity code, PNC No. and A/S No. are administrative and their local mandatory status is unknown.
- ETHNICITY CAPTURE. Whether the single 2026 'Ethnicity code' box means the PNC code, the self-defined 16+1 code, or both — the 2013 form had two separate dropdowns.
