# LER-1034 (MG4) — open questions for a practitioner

The MG4 field set is sourced and internally verified, but **no practitioner has confirmed it**, so
the template ships `verification: 'unverified'` and the form header says so. These are the questions
that decide whether it can be flipped to `verified`.

## Decisions taken during the rebuild (please confirm or correct)

1. **"Defence solicitor" was left out.** It is the only one of the 37 researched fields not marked
   `documented`, and our verifier confirmed it is *genuinely not a box* on the paper MG4 — it exists
   on the CM03 interface message instead. **Should the digital form capture it anyway?**
2. **The name is one box, not two.** The printed form has separate `Surname:` and `Forename(s):`
   boxes. `defendantName` could not be renamed or removed (a live draft stores a value under it) and
   adding both boxes alongside would capture the same name three times, so the split is described in
   the field's help text instead. **Should we migrate to two boxes?** That needs a data migration,
   not just a template change.
3. **`bailConditions` was retained but does not belong on this form.** The MG4 records
   *unconditional* bail only — conditions go on an MG4A. It is kept solely because a draft holds a
   value under that id. **Confirm it should be removed once that draft is resolved.**
4. **`courtName` and `hearingDate` are now optional.** On the paper form they sit inside the panel
   headed "FOR POST-CHARGE UNCONDITIONAL BAIL ONLY", so they only apply when bail was granted. They
   were previously required. **Confirm that is right.**
5. **"Specimen or additional charges" is a checkbox.** On the paper form this is a Manual of Guidance
   completion instruction ("mark clearly at the top of the form") rather than a printed box. We
   modelled it as a tick. **Is a checkbox the right representation?**

## Questions carried from the research

- **Which version of the MG4 is actually in use.** Ours is marked "2010/11" and carries the obsolete
  `RESTRICTED (when complete)` marking — under the Government Security Classification Policy this
  would now be OFFICIAL / OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE. The Home Office notes the NPCC has embargoed updates to
  the Manual of Guidance.
- **How much the form varies by force.** In practice the MG4 is generated by the force custody system
  rather than typed on the national template, so field order and extra boxes are force-specific.
  Fields plausibly added locally but absent nationally: place of birth, nationality, PNC ID, defence
  solicitor.
- **Which boxes custody sergeants treat as mandatory in practice** — particularly ethnicity code,
  interpreter language/dialect, the PPO and YO flags, and the "Officer in the case" block when the
  charging officer is also the OIC.
- **What belongs in the charge column** — verbatim PNLD wording, the CJS/CCCJS code plus particulars,
  or both; and how continuation sheets are numbered and cross-referenced locally.
- **The MG4 family split for written charges.** The 2010/11 templates divide MG4D ("Attendance
  Required") from MG4E ("Attendance Not Required"); the Home Office 2026 guidance instead describes
  MG4D as the youth form and MG4E as the adult form. Which convention applies must be settled before
  either is templated. Note the current template's old name promised "Written Requisition" and the
  rebuild drops it — written charges are a *separate form*.
- **Which caution is printed on the current form** — the standard s.34 CJPOA 1994 caution, or the
  restricted caution used where the detainee has not had access to legal advice.
- **Whether the four-copy distribution still applies** (File copy / Court copy / Custody record /
  Person charged) or digital service has replaced it.
- **Whether an MG4 is produced at all** where the force is on the Digital Case File or the police-CPS
  Two Way Interface. DG6 Annex 5 lists charge details as "Provided digitally or MG4".

## Structural limitation to note

The charge grid on the paper form is a table — `Sequential No. | Charge(s) | CCCJS Offence Code`. The
type system has no repeating group, so it is approximated as two parallel textareas (one charge per
line, codes in the same order). This is honest but not faithful; a repeating-group field type is the
proper fix and is tracked separately.
