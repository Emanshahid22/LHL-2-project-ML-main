## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want to be told when the case file changed after my form was populated, so that I can decide whether the form is still consistent with the file.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given a populated draft whose case has since been updated, when I open the form, then a banner says the case file has changed since the form was populated.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc02-autofill.spec.ts › `a case that changed after population surfaces the banner, and Re-run clears it`
- Given provenance for a filled field, when it is stored, then it records the case timestamp as at the moment of writing, which is what makes the comparison possible.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc02-autofill.spec.ts › `provenance records the source, the value and the case timestamp`
- Given the banner, when I re-run auto-fill, then the banner clears rather than persisting for the life of the draft.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc02-autofill.spec.ts › `a case that changed after population surfaces the banner, and Re-run clears it`

## Notes & Constraints

- Detection is a comparison, not a subscription: `caseChanged` is true when any provenance entry's
  `caseUpdatedAt` predates the case's current `updatedAt`.
- After a run, **every surviving provenance entry** has its timestamp refreshed to the case as just read.
  Without that the banner would stick forever after the first case edit.

- Resolver: `libs/shared/src/lib/autofill.ts` (pure). Service: `apps/api/src/drafts/drafts.service.ts`.
  Badges and notes: `apps/web/src/app/shared/dynamic-form/dynamic-form.ts`.
- **`mapsTo` only where the case datum genuinely IS the field.** An MG11 witness is not the defendant;
  MG16 `subjectName` stays unmapped pending a legal ruling. Never map signatures or non-OIC officers.
- Provenance matching is a `JSON.stringify` comparison, so dates must stay ISO strings in controls —
  `provideNativeDateAdapter` would silently kill every badge (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- **Additive only:** a field id is permanent; `valuesJson` and `autoFillJson` are both keyed by id.
