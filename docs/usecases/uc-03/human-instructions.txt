1. THE ONE BLOCKING ITEM — declaration wording sign-off
================================================================================

1.1  SIGN OFF THE STATUTORY DECLARATION WORDING  [memo Section B, final item]. This is the only
     acceptance criterion in the whole project recorded as BLOCKED, and it is blocked on a person, not
     on code. The memo asks for it in these words: "MG11 statutory declaration (provide the current
     official verbatim wording, s.9 CJA 1967 / s.5B MCA 1980 — the build carries a draft protected by
     an integrity hash)".

     The criterion: "MG11 declaration text matches the current official verbatim wording".

     Why nothing in the repo can close it: the scope document REQUIRES the wording to match the current
     official form, but does not reproduce that wording. There is no copy of the official MG11 in this
     repository to compare against. So the match cannot be proven here by any amount of testing.

     What IS already guaranteed, and does not need re-checking:
     - The wording exists in exactly ONE place: MG11_DECLARATION in libs/shared.
     - It is rendered read-only and is never stored per draft — a draft records only
       declarationConfirmed: true.
     - It is pinned by MG11_DECLARATION_SHA256, and the API hashes the constant on startup and REFUSES
       TO BOOT on a mismatch. There is no degraded mode.
     - No endpoint accepts declaration wording from any client, on any form (422 on `declarationText`).

     What the wording currently is: the s.9 CJA 1967 / s.5B MCA 1980 declaration as it ALREADY appeared
     in the MG11 template before this work started. Nobody has confirmed it against a current official
     form.

     WHO: someone with access to the current official MG11 — a senior solicitor or the client.
     Jira: sub-task LER-1200 exists for exactly this. Tickets LER-1077 (Senior solicitor review —
     wording) and LER-1078 (Senior solicitor review — output) are the review tickets, both blocked.

     IF THE WORDING NEEDS TO CHANGE: the constant and its SHA-256 must be updated in the SAME commit,
     or the API will not start. That friction is deliberate. The regeneration command is in a comment
     beside the constant.

2. PRACTITIONER (solicitor) — beyond the declaration
================================================================================

2.1  CONFIRM THE FIVE FLAGGED PHRASES ARE THE RIGHT FIVE. The scope names three ("I was told that",
     "I heard that", "In my opinion"); the build flags five, adding "I think" and "In my view".
     - Are those two right to flag? Are there phrases a solicitor would add?
     - Are the explanations correct? Each one says what to write INSTEAD, not just what is wrong, so a
       wrong explanation would actively mislead. They are in
       libs/shared/src/lib/narrative-flags.ts (NARRATIVE_PATTERNS) and are data, so adding a phrase is a
       one-line change plus a test.

2.2  CONFIRM THE VULNERABLE-WITNESS TEST. As built, a witness is treated as vulnerable if they were
     under 18 at the statement date, OR a special-measures category (vulnerable / intimidated /
     assessment) is selected.
     - Is under-18-at-statement-date the right test, or should it be age at the offence date, or at the
       likely trial date? The YJCEA 1999 s.16/s.17 categories are broader than age.
     - Are the three special-measures options the right list?
     - When the statement date is blank, age is assessed as at TODAY so vulnerability is never silently
       skipped. Confirm that fallback is acceptable.

2.3  VERIFY MG11's FIFTEEN FIELDS  [memo Section B lists MG11 and MG5 as "realistic but never
     practitioner-verified"]. MG11 carries NO `verification` marker, which means "not yet
     assessed" — NOT approved. It and MG5 are the quality bar but neither is practitioner-verified.
     Do not read the absent chip as a tick. See CLAUDE.md rule 2 and docs/HANDOVER.md §4.4.
