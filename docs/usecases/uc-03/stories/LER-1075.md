## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want flags to advise and never obstruct, so that I can record what a witness actually said and have my decision to proceed on the record.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given flagged phrases, when the narrative renders, then each is underlined in amber in place rather than listed elsewhere.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `marks align with the text they underline`
- Given flags are present, when I save, navigate between steps, or complete every step, then none of it is blocked.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `flags never block saving, navigating or completing`
- Given an MG11 narrative with flags, when the draft is saved, then the audit trail records how many flags there were and which distinct phrases they were.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `saving records the flags in the audit trail`
- Given detection fails, when the narrative renders, then it stays editable and a notice says flagging is unavailable.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `flags never block saving, navigating or completing`

## Notes & Constraints

- **Amber, not red.** Red is reserved for UC-07 sensitive material (CLAUDE.md rule 6); an advisory flag in
  red would read as an error.
- Non-blocking is a legal requirement, not a UX preference: a witness may genuinely have said something that
  reads as hearsay, and a form that refused to record it would be useless.
- The audit metadata records the **phrases and the count, never the narrative** — enough to show the decision
  to proceed was informed, without copying the statement into the audit trail.

- The wizard is a **shell around** `DynamicForm`, never a fork: `apps/web/.../form-fill/mg11-wizard/`
  passes `visibleFieldIds` and the renderer still owns the single `FormGroup`.
- Shared, pure: `libs/shared/src/lib/mg11-declaration.ts`, `narrative-flags.ts`, `mg11-witness.ts`.
- MG11 carries **no `verification` marker**, which means "not yet assessed" — it is the quality bar but is
  **not** practitioner-verified. Do not mark it verified because it looks good (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- **Additive only:** a field id is permanent; `valuesJson` is keyed by id.
