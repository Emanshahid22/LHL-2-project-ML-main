## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want the witness's identity and contact details captured first, so that the statement is attributable before I record what they say.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given step 1, when it renders, then it holds title, full name, date of birth, occupation, address and contact number — every field the scope names.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `step 1 holds witness details including the new title field`
- Given step 1, when it renders, then it also holds the witness-care fields (consent to attend court, special measures).
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `step 1 holds witness details including the new title field`
- Given the date of birth field, when I type a UK-format date, then it is accepted and stored as ISO.
  - Covered by: e2e/uk-dates.spec.ts › `typed DD/MM/YYYY is accepted and stored as ISO`
- Given a witness under 18 at the statement date, when step 1 is complete, then the vulnerable-witness route is triggered from this step.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `an under-18 date of birth reveals special measures and the indicator`

## Notes & Constraints

- The witness-care fields are an **addition beyond the scope's step-1 list**, which names six fields.
  They are on step 1 because special measures are assessed when the witness is identified, and because the
  vulnerability derivation reads them. Recorded in `open-questions.md` as an addition, not a departure.
- `title` was added in `templateVersion` 2 for this step.

- The wizard is a **shell around** `DynamicForm`, never a fork: `apps/web/.../form-fill/mg11-wizard/`
  passes `visibleFieldIds` and the renderer still owns the single `FormGroup`.
- Shared, pure: `libs/shared/src/lib/mg11-declaration.ts`, `narrative-flags.ts`, `mg11-witness.ts`.
- MG11 carries **no `verification` marker**, which means "not yet assessed" — it is the quality bar but is
  **not** practitioner-verified. Do not mark it verified because it looks good (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- **Additive only:** a field id is permanent; `valuesJson` is keyed by id.
