## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want a vulnerable witness identified automatically and shown persistently, so that special measures are considered rather than remembered.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given a witness under 18 at the statement date, when the date of birth is entered, then the Special measures applied field is revealed and a Vulnerable Witness chip appears in the header.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `an under-18 date of birth reveals special measures and the indicator`
- Given an adult witness with a special-measures category selected, when it is selected, then the same field and the same indicator appear.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `an adult with a special-measures category is treated the same`
- Given a vulnerable witness, when the draft is saved and reloaded, then the indicator and the derived `isVulnerableWitness` flag both survive.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `the indicator and derived flag survive a reload`
- Given the scope requires the indicator to persist to PDF output, when PDF exists, then the derived flag is already stored for it to read.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `the indicator and derived flag survive a reload` — PDF itself is UC-09; see `open-questions.md` G1.

## Notes & Constraints

- Two independent routes, either sufficient: a `specialMeasures` value of `vulnerable`, `intimidated` or
  `assessment`, **or** age under 18 at the statement date.
- `ageOnDate` assesses as at **today** when the statement date is blank, so an undated draft is never
  silently treated as an adult's. An unusable date of birth returns null rather than a guess, and the
  special-measures route still applies.
- The flag is **recomputed on every change, including back to false** — it does not latch. It is stored in
  `valuesJson` as a deliberate derived key, which `npm run orphan-scan` knows about so it is never reported
  as stranded data. UC-04 later reused this exact pattern for `hasSensitiveMaterial`.

- The wizard is a **shell around** `DynamicForm`, never a fork: `apps/web/.../form-fill/mg11-wizard/`
  passes `visibleFieldIds` and the renderer still owns the single `FormGroup`.
- Shared, pure: `libs/shared/src/lib/mg11-declaration.ts`, `narrative-flags.ts`, `mg11-witness.ts`.
- MG11 carries **no `verification` marker**, which means "not yet assessed" — it is the quality bar but is
  **not** practitioner-verified. Do not mark it verified because it looks good (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- **Additive only:** a field id is permanent; `valuesJson` is keyed by id.
