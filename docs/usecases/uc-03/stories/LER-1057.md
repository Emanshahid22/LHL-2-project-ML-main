## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want MG11 presented as a short sequence of steps, so that taking a statement follows the order a statement is actually taken rather than one long column of boxes.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given an MG11 draft, when it opens, then four steps are listed — Witness details, Statement narrative, Declaration, Signature and date.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `renders four steps, and only MG11 uses the wizard`
- Given any step, when I edit a field on it, then autosave persists from that step exactly as it does on a flat form.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `autosave works from every step`
- Given a part-completed statement, when I reopen the draft, then I land on the furthest incomplete step rather than back at step 1.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `resuming returns to the furthest incomplete step`
- Given a form that is not MG11, when it opens, then no wizard appears and the shared renderer is used.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `renders four steps, and only MG11 uses the wizard`

## Notes & Constraints

- The `FormGroup` is always built from the **whole** template; `visibleFieldIds` only narrows what is
  displayed. Hidden fields therefore keep their values and validators, and the header's overall
  *N of M required fields* count is unchanged from any other form.
- Step headers become clickable once visited; skipping ahead past unvisited steps is refused.
- `MG11_STEPS` lives in the wizard component, not in the shared template model — only MG11 has steps, and a
  `step` field on `FormFieldDefinition` would imply every form does.

- The wizard is a **shell around** `DynamicForm`, never a fork: `apps/web/.../form-fill/mg11-wizard/`
  passes `visibleFieldIds` and the renderer still owns the single `FormGroup`.
- Shared, pure: `libs/shared/src/lib/mg11-declaration.ts`, `narrative-flags.ts`, `mg11-witness.ts`.
- MG11 carries **no `verification` marker**, which means "not yet assessed" — it is the quality bar but is
  **not** practitioner-verified. Do not mark it verified because it looks good (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- **Additive only:** a field id is permanent; `valuesJson` is keyed by id.
