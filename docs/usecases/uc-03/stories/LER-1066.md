## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want the signature step to capture who signed, who took the statement and on what date, so that the served statement is properly attributed and dated.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given the declaration is confirmed, when I advance, then step 4 opens holding signature name, statement-taken-by and the statement date.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `cannot advance past the declaration until it is confirmed`
- Given step 4, when it renders, then a note states that space for a wet signature is reserved on PDF output.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `cannot advance past the declaration until it is confirmed`
- Given step 4, when I fill the signature name, then it autosaves and is persisted.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `autosave works from every step`
- Given the statement date field, when it renders, then it is DD/MM/YYYY on screen and ISO in storage, and a future date is refused.
  - Covered by: e2e/uk-dates.spec.ts › `every date field across all 11 forms uses the shared datepicker`
  - Covered by: e2e/uk-dates.spec.ts › `the no-future-date rule still applies`

## Notes & Constraints

- The wet-signature space itself is **UC-09** (PDF Generation & Formatting). Step 4 only records the
  requirement so it is not forgotten at layout time.
- `statementDate` is also an input to the vulnerability derivation: age is assessed as at the statement
  date, falling back to today when it is blank.

- The wizard is a **shell around** `DynamicForm`, never a fork: `apps/web/.../form-fill/mg11-wizard/`
  passes `visibleFieldIds` and the renderer still owns the single `FormGroup`.
- Shared, pure: `libs/shared/src/lib/mg11-declaration.ts`, `narrative-flags.ts`, `mg11-witness.ts`.
- MG11 carries **no `verification` marker**, which means "not yet assessed" — it is the quality bar but is
  **not** practitioner-verified. Do not mark it verified because it looks good (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- **Additive only:** a field id is permanent; `valuesJson` is keyed by id.
