## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want the wizard to stop me reaching the signature step before the declaration is confirmed, so that a statement cannot be signed with the declaration skipped.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given step 3 with the box unticked, when I press Next, then I stay on step 3 and an inline reminder tells me to confirm the declaration.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `cannot advance past the declaration until it is confirmed`
- Given the box unticked, when I try to jump to step 4 by its header, then I am returned to step 3 rather than allowed past.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `cannot advance past the declaration until it is confirmed`
- Given I tick the box, when I press Next, then step 4 opens.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `cannot advance past the declaration until it is confirmed`
- Given the reminder is showing, when I then tick the box, then the reminder clears without needing another Next.
  - Covered by: e2e/uc03-mg11-wizard.spec.ts › `cannot advance past the declaration until it is confirmed`

## Notes & Constraints

- This is the **only** gate in the wizard. Steps 1, 2 and 4 may be left incomplete, because a statement is
  often taken out of order and blocking on an unfinished narrative would be obstructive. The declaration is
  different in kind: it is the legal predicate for the signature.
- Saving is never blocked, even at the gate — the draft is always persistable.

- The wizard is a **shell around** `DynamicForm`, never a fork: `apps/web/.../form-fill/mg11-wizard/`
  passes `visibleFieldIds` and the renderer still owns the single `FormGroup`.
- Shared, pure: `libs/shared/src/lib/mg11-declaration.ts`, `narrative-flags.ts`, `mg11-witness.ts`.
- MG11 carries **no `verification` marker**, which means "not yet assessed" — it is the quality bar but is
  **not** practitioner-verified. Do not mark it verified because it looks good (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- **Additive only:** a field id is permanent; `valuesJson` is keyed by id.
