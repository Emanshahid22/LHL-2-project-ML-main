1. PRACTITIONER (solicitor) — the critical path
================================================================================

1.1  VERIFY THE TEMPLATE FIELD SETS  [memo Section B is the agenda for this, form by form]. This is
     the single biggest outstanding item in the project, and it lands on UC-01 because UC-01 renders
     the templates and carries the marker.
     - NO template is currently marked `verification: 'verified'`. Six carry
       `verification: 'unverified'` (MG3, MG4, MG6, MG12, MG15, MG16) and render a persistent
       "Unverified template — pending practitioner sign-off" chip on the form header.
     - MG5 and MG11 carry NO marker at all. They are the quality bar but they are NOT verified either
       — do not read the absent marker as approval. This is standing decision 4 in docs/HANDOVER.md.
     - What to do: work through docs/review/mg-field-review.csv per form (~15 min/form). For each
       field, confirm it is a real box on the current official form, and confirm its label.
     - Then, and only then, ask an engineer to flip that template's `verification` to 'verified'.
       Flipping it is a one-line change; deciding it is not.

1.2  ANSWER docs/review/mg-form-summary.csv FIRST (9 rows, ~15 minutes)  [memo A1, A2, A3, A4]. It
     asks a blunter question than the field review: is each MG code even the right form? Research says
     MG2 is the Special Measures Assessment and MG14 is the Conditional Caution, which would mean two
     of the eleven forms in the picker are mislabelled. Answering this before the field review avoids
     reviewing fields on a form that turns out to be the wrong form.

1.3  CONFIRM THE HELP TEXT IS SAFE TO RELY ON. Every field carries `helpText` shown behind its `?`
     icon, and much of it was written from official guidance rather than from practice. A solicitor
     should confirm that no help text advises something wrong.

2. DECISION-MAKER
================================================================================

2.1  IS MG1 IN SCOPE?  [memo A1 — full evidence and three options are stated there]. This is a direct
     conflict inside the scope document and the product cannot resolve it.
     - The UC-01 Definition of Done says "All 11 MG forms are available and load correctly".
     - Research found MG1 is not in the current MG suite at all: 0 of 7 fields corroborated, verdict
       not-usable (docs/mg-form-research-findings.md).
     - MG1 is currently retained: it is in the picker and it is the fixture form for around 20 E2E
       assertions.
     - Decide: keep MG1 (and accept that the picker offers a form that may not exist), or drop it
       (and accept that "all 11" becomes "all 10" and around 20 fixtures move to another form).
     - This is why acceptance criterion #1 is recorded as Partial rather than Covered.

2.2  MG2 AND MG14 DESIGNATIONS, AND THE FATE OF THEIR LIVE DRAFTS  [memo A2, A3 — which record one
     live MG2 draft and three MG14 drafts]. If MG2 becomes the Special
     Measures Assessment and MG14 the Conditional Caution, their field sets change wholesale. Drafts
     already exist against the current ids. Run `npm run orphan-scan` BEFORE any such change — it
     reports exactly what would be stranded and exits 1 if anything would be.

2.3  SHOULD `templateVersion` BE HONOURED? Today a draft records the version it was created against
     but renders against the current field set (form-fill-page.ts loads by formCode). That is safe
     only while every template change is additive. Options: honour the recorded version and render
     old drafts against their own field set, or formally adopt forward-migration. Roadmap item 4.

MEMO DECISIONS THAT LAND ON UC-01. All four form-identity decisions are UC-01 decisions, because
UC-01 is what puts eleven forms in the picker and renders whichever field sets they carry.

A1  Does the product carry MG1 at all? THE decision for this use case. MG1 is absent from the Home
    Office register (Criminal Casefiles v2.0, which begins at MG02), absent from DG6 6th ed., and
    returns zero occurrences in the 2011 Manual of Guidance; none of its 7 fields corroborated. It is
    in the picker today and is the fixture form for around 20 E2E assertions. This is precisely why
    acceptance criterion #1 ("All 11 MG forms are available and load correctly") is Partial.
    Memo options: (a) remove it and amend the criterion; (b) keep it as a clearly-labelled
    non-statutory local cover sheet; (c) replace the slot with another form.
    Blocks LER-1031, the sign-off scope, and the UC-09 layout binding for this slot.

A2  MG2 designation. Officially the Special Measures Assessment; our MG2 holds taped-interview
    content that officially belongs on MG15. The memo records ONE LIVE DRAFT under the current field
    ids, so run `npm run orphan-scan` before any re-designation — it reports exactly what would be
    stranded and exits 1 if anything would be. Blocks LER-1032.

A3  MG14 designation. Officially the Conditional Caution; our MG14 holds eyewitness/identification
    content, which officially belongs on an MG11. The memo records THREE LIVE DRAFTS — same
    orphan-scan warning. Part of the public research traced to an AI-generated document and was
    struck, so a genuine blank MG14 is needed before any rebuild. Blocks LER-1037.

A4  Is MG16 one form or two? For UC-01 this is a template-shape question: one template carrying both
    field sets with relabelled sections, or a split with a new ebc003 template. The same decision
    governs UC-02's subject-name auto-fill rule — see docs/usecases/uc-02/human-instructions.txt.
