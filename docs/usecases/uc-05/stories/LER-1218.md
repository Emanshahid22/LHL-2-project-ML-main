# LER-1218 — Statement date against the hearing date, advisory

_UC-05 Field Validation · epic: `docs/usecases/uc-05/epic.md` · PRD: `docs/usecases/uc-05/prd.md`_

_DevOps Bot ticket **LER-1218** (Story, To Do) · build stage order: story 12 of 16._

## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want an unusual statement date queried rather than rejected, so that a further statement taken after the first hearing still goes on the file.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given an MG11 draft linked to a case with a listed hearing, when `statementDate` is later than the case's `nextHearingAt`, then an **advisory** finding appears in the Consistency Issues panel.
- Given that advisory, when I save the draft, then it saves, and no control is invalidated.
- Given an MG11 draft, when `statementDate` is earlier than the witness's date of birth, then an **error** finding appears — that ordering is impossible, not merely unusual.
- Given a case with no listed hearing, when the check runs, then it is suppressed silently.
- Given both findings, when I correct the offending date, then they clear automatically.

## Notes & Constraints

- **Why advisory and not error:** a statement taken after the first hearing is ordinary practice — further
  statements are served throughout a case. Flagging it as an error would train users to ignore the panel,
  which is how a validation feature becomes worthless.
- The date-of-birth comparison on the same field is an error, which makes this story the clearest
  illustration that severity belongs to the **rule**, not to the field or the form.
- `statementDate` also feeds UC-03's vulnerability derivation (age at the statement date). Do not change its
  storage or its adapter.

- Rule engine: `libs/shared/src/lib/validation.ts` (new, pure). Types:
  `libs/shared/src/lib/form-template.types.ts`. Rules on fields: `form-templates.ts`.
- **No invented format.** A format may be `error` severity only if its provenance is `documented` with a
  cited source. Everything else is advisory. This is enforced by `severityFor()` and by
  `npm run conformance` — not by good intentions.
- **Additive only:** never rename or delete a field id; bump `templateVersion` on any template whose rules
  change; run `npm run orphan-scan` around any id change.
- Reuse the shared renderer. Do not special-case a form code; a rule is declared on the field that owns it.
- After changing `libs/shared`, **restart the API** — the nest watcher does not pick up shared-lib rebuilds.
- Full mechanism and business rules: `docs/usecases/uc-05/prd.md` (§4 and §8).
