## User Story
As a defence solicitor, I want the reminder panel to link to the governing procedure rules, so that the authoritative text is one click away from the material it governs.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given the PII panel, when it renders for a permitted user, then it carries a link labelled as the Criminal Procedure Rules and Practice Directions index, opening in a new tab (`rel="noopener"`).
- Given the link's target, when it ships, then it is the official gov.uk index — a real, verifiable destination — and the exact practice-direction reference remains open-questions Q1, stated as such in the panel copy.
- Given the constant `PII_PRACTICE_DIRECTION`, when a practitioner answers Q1, then retargeting is a one-constant data change.

## Notes & Constraints
- Explicit scope requirement. Do NOT guess a specific practice-direction number to look more precise —
  a wrong citation on this surface is worse than a general one (rule 1 applied to references).
